How it works: every movie is a binary vector of tags. Your profile is a weighted vector (interests = 1.0, tags from liked movies = 0.6). Movies are ranked by cosine similarity between your profile and the movie vector: dot(u, m) / (|u| × |m|). Movies you liked are excluded.
